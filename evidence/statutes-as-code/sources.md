  real problems, outside Q1 to Q5.
- LogicLease (arXiv:2502.09204) and the insurance study (arXiv:2502.17638): same pattern as Jurayj 2025 with smaller evidence.
- Lean results outside law (phase retrieval, attention, provers on miniF2F): no legal content.
- The Envisioning page on rules as code: an aggregator, unchecked claims.
